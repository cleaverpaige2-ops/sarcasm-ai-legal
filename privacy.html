Privacy Policy for SarcasmAI

Last updated: August 20, 2026

SarcasmAI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the SarcasmAI mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

a. User-Provided Content

SarcasmAI may process text you enter into the App, including messages, prompts, and requests for generated responses or emoji-style sticker images.

Favorites or saved content may be stored locally on your device so you can access saved replies, templates, or stickers later.

SarcasmAI does not require users to create an account, and we do not require real names, phone numbers, or email addresses to use the App.

b. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical and usage information, such as feature usage, request frequency, app errors, device type, operating system version, and anonymous device identifiers used for field-test credits, usage limits, abuse prevention, debugging, and app stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Generate sarcastic replies, templates, and emoji-style sticker images
  • Operate and improve the App
  • Improve humor quality, tone, performance, and reliability
  • Track field-test credits and usage limits
  • Prevent abuse, exploitation, or misuse
  • Maintain App stability, security, and debugging

3. AI Processing

User input may be sent to our backend servers and processed by third-party AI services, such as Google Generative AI, solely to generate replies, templates, or emoji-style sticker images requested by the user.

We do not sell or rent user content. We do not use user content for advertising.

We do not intentionally store raw message input permanently. Temporary logs may be retained for a limited time when needed for debugging, abuse prevention, moderation, security, or service reliability.

4. Data Retention

Generated responses, favorites, and sticker images may be stored locally on your device.

Temporary server logs and usage records may be retained for system integrity, debugging, credit tracking, abuse prevention, and security. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

SarcasmAI may use third-party services for AI generation, backend hosting, analytics, crash reporting, app stability, and security. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

When you copy, save, or share generated content outside the App, that action is controlled by you and may be subject to the privacy practices of the receiving app or service.

6. Content Responsibility

You are responsible for any content you choose to create, copy, save, or share. SarcasmAI is designed for humor and entertainment, not harassment, abuse, threats, or harm.

7. Children’s Privacy

SarcasmAI is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information. Because SarcasmAI does not currently require user accounts, some information may only be associated with an anonymous device identifier.

You may clear locally stored favorites or app data through your device settings. You may also contact us with privacy questions or deletion requests.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
