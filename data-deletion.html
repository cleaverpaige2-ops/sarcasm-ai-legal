Data Deletion Request for SarcasmAI

Last updated: September 3, 2026

SarcasmAI allows users to request deletion of data associated with their app usage when deletion is possible. SarcasmAI does not currently require users to create an account, so some data may only be associated with an anonymous device identifier.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your SarcasmAI data, email us at:

Email: DogsofWarHots@proton.me

Please include the following information in your request:

  • State that you are requesting SarcasmAI data deletion.
  • Tell us whether you are using the Android test version of the app.
  • If possible, include the approximate date you used the app.
  • Do not include passwords, private messages, or sensitive personal information.

What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on what data is available, we may delete or anonymize:

  • Temporary server logs related to app usage
  • Anonymous device-based usage records
  • Field-test credit or usage records associated with the device
  • Debugging or diagnostic records that can reasonably be associated with the request

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Some SarcasmAI data, such as favorites, saved replies, and saved stickers, may be stored locally on your device. You can delete locally stored app data by clearing SarcasmAI app storage through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Retention

Temporary server logs and usage records may be retained for a limited time for security, debugging, abuse prevention, field-test credit tracking, and service reliability. When data is no longer needed, it may be deleted or anonymized.
